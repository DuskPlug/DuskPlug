DuskPlug

A free, open-source tray and menu bar app for Tuya and Smart Life smart plugs on Windows, Linux, and macOS. Turn your desk lamp on at sunset and off at sunrise — no Home Assistant required.

Why DuskPlug?

  • Smart Mode — plug on at sunset, off at sunrise from your location
  • Schedule Mode — fixed daily on/off times, including overnight windows
  • Manual control — toggle from the tray or menu bar
  • Multi-device — plugs and dimmable bulbs, each with its own mode
  • Your Tuya project only — credentials stay on your PC
  • Lock-off — optional turn-off when the computer is locked or sleeping
  • Screen brightness — optional automatic monitor dimming through the day
