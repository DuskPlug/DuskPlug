DuskPlug

Why DuskPlug?

  • Smart Mode — plug on at sunset, off at sunrise from your location
  • Schedule Mode — fixed daily on/off times, including overnight windows
  • Manual control — toggle from the system tray icon
  • Multi-device — plugs and dimmable bulbs, each with its own mode
  • Your Tuya project only — credentials stay on your PC
  • Lock-off — optional turn-off when the PC is locked

Getting started

You need a free Tuya Developer Platform project linked to the same Smart Life or Tuya phone app that controls your plug. The one-time setup takes about 10 minutes.
