Screen brightness

These settings control your local monitor backlight. They do nothing until you turn on Screen brightness → Automatic from the tray menu (right-click the DuskPlug icon).

Night and day levels

When Automatic is on from the tray menu, DuskPlug adjusts brightness between these levels using your home location above. Without a window direction below, it switches at sunset and sunrise. If a device uses Schedule mode, the night level applies during scheduled “on” hours instead.

Window direction

Optional. When set, Automatic blends smoothly between night and day based on where the sun is relative to your main window through the day and season. Leave as “Not set” for a simple sunset/sunrise switch instead.

Soft keeps brightness higher when the sun is near your window; Sharp only reaches the day level when the sun is more directly on the glass.
